Men’s Masters

Men’s Masters hockey at Mentone — competitive games across Hockey Victoria’s Masters grades, for players 35 and over. Age brackets typically cover 35+, 45+, 55+ and 65+.

No set midweek session — game day is the go, so contact the coordinator to get started. The social side of Masters is strong — come for the hockey, stay for everything else.

Women’s Masters

Women’s Masters hockey at Mentone — a welcoming, competitive environment for women 35 and over. Play at your level, with people who love the game as much as you do.

Whether you’re returning to hockey after a break or have been playing for decades, you’ll find a home in the Women’s Masters section at Mentone.

Why Masters?

Still got it. Come prove it.

Real competition

Hockey Victoria runs proper Masters competitions with age-grouped grades. It’s competitive, well-organised and seriously fun.

Great social side

The Masters community at Mentone is tight-knit and welcoming. Club rooms open after every home game — come down and see for yourself.

One club community

Masters is part of the whole club — same presentation night, same events, same Panthers pride. You’re not in a silo; you’re part of something bigger.
