First taste of hockey

A fun, friendly first taste of hockey.

Six free Sunday clinics run by Mentone Hockey Club volunteers, for kids aged 5–15 of all abilities. Fun first — skills through games, building confidence and making friends. No experience or gear needed to start.

2026 Term 4 Beginner Clinics are on now — Sunday mornings, 10:00–11:00am at Mentone Hockey Ground. Six clinics: 18 and 25 October, then 8, 15, 22 and 29 November. No session on 1 November, the Sunday before Melbourne Cup. They’re free, but places need to be booked — register through the club portal below.

  • Free — six Sunday clinics run by Mentone Hockey Club volunteers
  • All equipment provided and uniforms sorted — just wear sports clothes and runners
  • Ages 5–15, all abilities, everybody welcome
  • Small groups, lots of touches, plenty of smiles
  • A clear pathway into our Juniors teams when they’re ready

Our approach

Fun first. Skills that stick.

Our junior program promotes fun, enjoyment, teamwork and a positive attitude — while helping players develop strong skills and a love of the game.

Training combines technical coaching with a small-game approach, building individual ability, teamwork and game awareness in a positive and supportive environment.

Senior players from both the men’s and women’s sections actively support the junior program — mentoring players and helping make the transition into senior hockey smooth and welcoming.
