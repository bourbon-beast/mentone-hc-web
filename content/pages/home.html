New Players

A fun, friendly first taste of hockey.

Six free Sunday clinics run by Mentone Hockey Club volunteers, for kids aged 5–15 of all abilities. Fun first — learning skills through games, building confidence and making friends. Clinics run 18 October to 29 November; no session on 1 November, the Sunday before Melbourne Cup. Free, but places need booking.

  • Free — six Sunday clinics run by Mentone Hockey Club volunteers
  • All equipment provided — sticks, balls, shinpads
  • Small groups, lots of touches, plenty of smiles
  • A clear pathway into our Juniors teams when they're ready
